## Data gaps / caveats

1. **Stars for transfers & upperclassmen:** Most portal arrivals do not have a current 247/On3 “roster star.” Only HS-class ratings found in public bios/class boards are filled; others are **Not listed** (not invented).
2. **Depth chart volatility:** SI Sep 1 opener chart differs from Aug preseason chart (WR Z/slot, kicker). Week-to-week official two-deeps may change after Portland State.
3. **Jersey conflicts:** Some numbers shared or differ across goaztecs / Ourlads / SI (e.g., Rahman #45 vs #59; Acevedo #3 vs #13). Prefer official athletics site for jersey when conflicting.
4. **Incomplete star coverage for 2024–2025 HS classes:** Returning sophomores/juniors signed in prior cycles often lack easy public star tables in this research pass.
5. **Marshel Sanders / other signees:** On3 lists additional 2026 commits (e.g., Marshel Sanders) who may or may not appear on the current athletics roster page — not forced onto the roster table without official listing.
6. **Ourlads defense table** partially failed to render in fetch; defense two-deep taken primarily from SI Aug 4, 2026 article.
7. **Player count:** Official goaztecs.com 2026 roster lists **~110 players** (card view). Full table above covers all major position groups from that page; a few walk-ons may appear only in print views.

**Approximate player count in this file:** ~110 (aligned with official roster).
