## Data gaps / caveats

1. **No full official public two-deep** found as of access date. Depth chart is a synthesis of Ourlads + beat projections + confirmed QB announcement. Position battles remain fluid (RB, OL, several WR/TE spots).
2. **QB:** Braden Atkinson **confirmed** starter (Aug. 24, 2026). Earlier mid-camp projections naming Maalik Murphy are outdated.
3. **Recruiting stars:** Individually verified: Maalik Murphy **5★** (2022 HS composite), Cornell Hatcher Jr. **3★**, Aeryn Hampton cited as former **4★**. On3 aggregate: 5 four-stars on roster — other 4★ names not confirmed in free HTML. Remainder **Not listed**.
4. **Recruitment/class year:** True freshmen marked **2026** when official year is Fr. Most upperclassmen/transfers **Not listed**; Murphy **2022** HS class confirmed.
5. **Eligibility murky:** Jaheim Patterson (DB) and Van Wells (OL/C) practicing but eligibility unresolved per Herald Pioneer — may or may not be available Week 1.
6. **Legal/status notes (public media):** Aiden Sullivan facing felony assault charge (playing-time unclear); Exodus Ayers facing multiple charges (status unclear) — reported by Herald Pioneer, not invented here.
7. **Hometown/HS variances:** Occasional On3 vs official mismatches (e.g., Xavyion Noland hometown); **official athletics roster preferred**.
8. **Specialists:** Kick/punt starter roles not firmly confirmed in free sources; multiple Ks listed (Ojeda, Oh, Smith).
9. **Tygee Hill:** Appears on some Ourlads NT lines in search snippets but **not** on official osubeavers.com 2026 roster fetch — omitted from full roster table.
