## Data gaps / caveats

1. **Recruiting stars:** Individual 247/On3 composite stars were verified only for a minority of players (e.g., Jayden Mandal 3★/84, Rayshon Luke ~4★/93 HS, Brayden Walton 3★ class of 2025, Josiah Freeman 3★ JUCO). All others marked **Not listed** — do not invent ratings. On3 aggregate implies most rated players are 3★.
2. **Recruitment/class year:** True freshmen listed as **2026** when official year is Fr. Returning/transfer class years mostly **Not listed** unless confirmed (Mandal 2023, Walton 2025, Freeman 2022, Luke ~2021). Eligibility year (Fr/So/Jr/Sr) is in Notes.
3. **Defensive line starters:** Collegian official two-deep article emphasized skill positions/secondary/LBs/specialists; did not publish a full DL two-deep in the same detail. DL names appear on roster; Jahzon Jacks is a captain.
4. **Injuries (reported):** Brandon Ramirez out for season (leg, Spring camp); Edward Fonua and Jomarion Briggs listed INJ on Ourlads (2026-09-03); Brock Lium injury noted by Collegian.
5. **Name spelling:** Collegian sometimes spells "Jahil" / "Tytys" / "Ginor" / "Rollan"; official roster uses **Jahlil McClain**, **Tytus Khajavi**, **Gino Quinones**, **Rolan Fullwood** — official spelling preferred.
6. **Loyall Mouzon hometown:** Official lists Rochester, NY with Millikan HS (Long Beach-area school) — retained as published; possible data quirk.
7. Cross-checked official roster vs Ourlads (updated 2026-09-03); minor HT/WT/hometown variances noted in Notes where relevant.
