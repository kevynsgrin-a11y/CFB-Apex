## Data gaps / caveats

1. **No official public Week 1 depth chart** — Boise State and Oregon both declined to publish two-deeps; starter list relies on Danielson press comments + Ourlads/SI projections.
2. **Recruiting stars incomplete for upperclassmen/transfers** — Verified 247/On3 composite stars are included primarily for the 2026 signing class and selected known returners (e.g., Madsen 3★/2022, Riley 3★/2024, Gaines 3★/2024). Many 2022–2025 signees and portal additions are marked **Not listed** rather than guessed.
3. **Hometown discrepancies** — Official site lists Rasean Jones hometown as Baker City, Ore. with Rocky Mountain HS (Meridian, ID); Ourlads lists Meridian, ID. Both noted.
4. **Class year definition** — "Class Year" = high school recruiting class / signing year when known; true freshmen without a published rating still show **2026** as recruiting class.
5. **Jersey overlaps** — Multiple players share numbers (common on large FBS rosters); listed as published.
6. **Ourlads vs coach** — Ourlads still had Jason Steele listed ahead of Stewart Taufa at center as of Aug 30; coach later named **Taufa** the Week 1 center and **Rasmussen** LG.
