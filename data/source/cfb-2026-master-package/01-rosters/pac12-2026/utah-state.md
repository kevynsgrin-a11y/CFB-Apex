## Data gaps / caveats

1. **No single official published Week-1 depth chart** from Utah State Athletics was retrieved; athletics roster page returned a JS shell. Depth is **projected** (Big Blue Aug 31, 2026; HERO; Ourlads May 2026).
2. **OL alignment conflict:** Big Blue puts Jimmy Liston at LT and Kadin Lynch at C; Ourlads/HERO put Liston at C with Randle/Wilfred at tackle. Both are cited; do not invent a single “official” OL.
3. **WR starter conflict:** Big Blue leads X with Eli Wood; Ourlads/HERO often lead with Anthony Garcia / Demick Starling. Garcia is the clearest returning offensive skill starter.
4. **Stars:** Many walk-ons, JUCO, and lower-profile transfers have **Not listed**. Big Blue “NR” treated as Not listed / unrated. Specialist “4.5★/5★” kicking ratings on Big Blue are **not** standard composite football stars — flagged above for Afrookhteh.
5. **Class years:** Exact HS recruiting years are incomplete for many multi-school transfers; used “Not listed” + transfer year where HS class not verified.
6. **Roster churn:** Wikipedia/Ourlads still show some players who appear on outgoing transfer lists or may be inactive (e.g., mission delays for Jesse King / Ifo Pili Jr.). Prefer CBS + Big Blue + signing release for active 2026 participation.
7. **Marcus McKenzie** appears on official signing list; limited presence on depth charts — status unclear for Week 1.
8. **Player count:** Approximately **110–120** unique names appear across CBS + Ourlads + Big Blue + Wiki; after deduping and noting uncertain/mission/outgoing overlaps, **~95–105** are treated as active roster candidates for 2026. Exact scholarship/count not published in sources used.
