4. **Beau Sparks** and several JC/portal specialists lack published HS star ratings in sources used → **Not listed**.

6. **Wikipedia** portal out list includes many 2025 departures (e.g., Tyler Robles K to Washington) — useful for understanding roster turnover, not for inventing current players.
7. **Player count:** Official txst.com table lists **~108 players**.
