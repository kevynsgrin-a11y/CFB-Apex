## Data gaps / caveats

1. **No official WSU Week-1 depth chart** — program declined to release one before Apple Cup; only **Caden Pinnick** confirmed as a starter by coaches. Two-deep is **Spokesman-Review projection** (camp observations), cross-checked with Daily Evergreen.
2. **Recruiting stars sparse:** Official roster and Ourlads do not publish composite stars. On3/ESPN provide ratings for some **2026 HS signees** only. Vast majority of roster (especially transfers) → **Not listed**. Prefer honesty over invention.
3. **HS recruiting class years** rarely published on athletics pages for upperclassmen/transfers → many “Not listed” with eligibility class instead.
4. **May vs August OL:** Ourlads (May 2026) OL alignments disagree with August camp projections; August sources preferred for Week-1 starters.
5. **Injuries:** Leckner and Cedarland out to open season — TE depth thinner than roster implies.
6. **Eligibility / portal edge cases:** Leo Pulalasi appears on Wiki outgoing “withdrawn” and still on official roster; Soni Finau eligibility mentioned by Moore (Spokesman) but not fully clarified in sources used.
7. **Commits vs active roster:** Some On3/247 2026 commits (e.g., Bryce Heckard, Cooper Daines, Nathaniel Salmon) were **not clearly present** on the official wsucougars.com roster page at access time — listed with “confirm” notes rather than forced onto depth chart.
8. **Player count:** Official athletics roster scrape yields approximately **100–105** players with jersey/bio entries (including specialists). After noting unconfirmed signees, treat **~100** as the public active roster size.
