## 2. Methodology and Primary Source Types

Research for each team was compiled from publicly available sources accessed on or about **September 4–5, 2026**. Primary source types used across the conference package:

1. **Official athletics department rosters** (school `.edu` / athletics sites) — primary authority for jersey numbers, names, positions, hometowns/high schools, and academic eligibility class when published.
2. **Ourlads.com** depth charts and numeric/alphabetical rosters — secondary depth and hometown cross-checks (update stamps vary by school; often May–August 2026).
3. **Beat / local media depth charts and coaching comments** — e.g., SI On Campus affiliates, *The Collegian* (Fresno State), Spokesman-Review (Washington State), Herald Pioneer (Oregon State), Big Blue USU Aggie News, Dave Campbell’s Texas Football, Idaho Press / Yahoo, Deseret News / HJ News / KSL.
4. **Recruiting databases and class trackers** — 247Sports, On3, ESPN recruiting, CFBTrack, school signing-day releases — used **only** when a specific rating was publicly verified for a named player.
5. **Secondary cross-checks** — CBS Sports team rosters, Wikipedia season pages (portal context), HERO Sports previews.

**Policy enforced throughout:** If a recruiting star, high-school class year, or starter designation could not be verified in the sources consulted, the field is marked **Not listed** or the chart is labeled **projected**. No ratings or starter assignments were invented.

## 3. Cross-Conference Caveats

Readers should treat the attached materials as a **research snapshot**, not as an official Pac-12 or NCAA publication. The following limitations apply conference-wide:

| Caveat | Implication |
| --- | --- |
| **Stars incomplete for transfers and upperclassmen** | Composite / consensus stars are filled primarily for verified 2026 (and some earlier) high-school signees and a minority of returners with published bios. Most portal arrivals and multi-year veterans are **Not listed**. |
| **Many depth charts are projected, not official** | Only Fresno State is documented with an official coaching-staff two-deep (via *The Collegian*). Several programs declined public Week-1 charts (notably Boise State and Washington State). Other charts synthesize Ourlads + beat projections + confirmed starter announcements. |
| **Jersey mismatches** | Numbers frequently differ across athletics sites, Ourlads, and media charts (shared numbers on large FBS rosters; SI/Ourlads vs official conflicts). Prefer the school athletics roster for jersey when sources conflict. |
| **Position-battle volatility** | Open competitions (e.g., Colorado State QB; Oregon State RB/WR/OL; Utah State OL/WR alignments) mean Week-1 actual lineups may diverge from mid/late-August projections. |
| **Roster churn** | Heavy portal turnover (especially Colorado State under Mora) means late cuts, injuries, and eligibility rulings can change counts after the research date. |
| **Hometown / HS quirks** | Occasional official-site anomalies (blank HS fields, mismatched city/school pairs) are preserved as published rather than “corrected.” |

## 5. How to Read the Attached Team Files

Each school file in `/workspace/pac12-2026/` follows a consistent research structure:

1. **Header** — Conference affiliation, head coach, home venue (when stated), access/research date.
2. **Sources table** — URLs and access dates for every primary source used for that school.
3. **Starter Depth Chart** — Offense, defense, and specialists, with an explicit **projected vs official** caveat at the top of the section.
4. **Full Roster** — Player tables with columns for Name, Stars, Class Year, Position, High School / Hometown, and Notes. Gaps are preserved as **Not listed**.
5. **Data gaps / caveats** — School-specific limitations (injuries, eligibility murk, jersey conflicts, missing DL rows, etc.).

### Reading conventions

- **★** on a depth chart marks a starter (confirmed or projected, per that file’s caveat).
- **Not listed** means the field was not verified — it does **not** mean “unrated” in a recruiting-service sense, nor that the player is unimportant.
- **Class Year** generally means high-school recruiting / signing year when known; academic eligibility (Fr/So/Jr/Sr) often appears in Notes.
- Prefer **official athletics spelling** when media spellings diverge (documented in Fresno State and other caveats).
