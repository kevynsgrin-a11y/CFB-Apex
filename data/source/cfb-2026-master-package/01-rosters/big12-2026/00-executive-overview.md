## 2. Methodology and Primary Source Types

Research for each team was compiled from publicly available sources accessed on or about **September 4–5, 2026**. Primary source types used across the conference package:

1. **Official athletics department rosters** (school `.edu` / athletics sites) — primary authority for jersey numbers, names, positions, hometowns/high schools, and academic eligibility class when published.
2. **Official or beat-reported depth charts** — e.g., Arizona Week-1 PDF vs NAU; Arizona State Week-1 chart via azcentral; Baylor Week-1 PDF vs Auburn; BYU Aug 31 release; Colorado game-day chart vs Georgia Tech (DNVR); Iowa State Game 1 PDF vs SEMO; Kansas Aug 31 chart; Kansas State Week-1 two-deep (CJ Online); Oklahoma State program chart (Pistols Firing); West Virginia Week-1 chart (SI / Hail WV); UCF initial team release; Texas Tech coach presser confirmations.
3. **Ourlads.com** depth charts and numeric/alphabetical rosters — secondary depth and hometown cross-checks (update stamps vary by school; often August–September 2026).
4. **Beat / local media depth charts and coaching comments** — e.g., Cincinnati Enquirer / Yahoo (Cincinnati); SI Houston projected offense; Lubbock Avalanche-Journal (Texas Tech); Daytona Beach News-Journal (UCF); Deseret News (Utah); SicEm365 (Baylor); Bring On The Cats (Kansas State).
5. **Recruiting databases and class trackers** — 247Sports, On3, ESPN recruiting, CFBTrack, school signing-day releases — used **only** when a specific rating was publicly verified for a named player.
6. **Secondary cross-checks** — ESPN / CBS roster pages, Wikipedia season pages (portal/schedule context), school game notes PDFs where cited in team files.

**Policy enforced throughout:** If a recruiting star, high-school class year, or starter designation could not be verified in the sources consulted, the field is marked **Not listed** or the chart is labeled **projected**. No ratings or starter assignments were invented.

## 3. Cross-Conference Caveats

Readers should treat the attached materials as a **research snapshot**, not as an official Big 12 Conference or NCAA publication. The following limitations apply conference-wide:

| Caveat | Implication |
| --- | --- |
| **Stars incomplete for transfers and upperclassmen** | Composite / consensus stars are filled primarily for verified 2026 (and some earlier) high-school signees and a minority of returners/transfers with published bios. Most portal arrivals and multi-year veterans are **Not listed**. |
| **Many depth charts are projected or mixed, not fully official** | Arizona, Arizona State, Baylor, BYU, Colorado, Iowa State, Kansas, Kansas State, Oklahoma State, and West Virginia have **OFFICIAL** Week-1 / opener two-deeps (athletics PDF or program chart via beat). Cincinnati, Texas Tech, UCF, and Utah are **MIXED** (partial coach/game confirmation + projected remainder). Houston and TCU charts are primarily **PROJECTED** (Ourlads and/or beat; no athletics Week-1 two-deep located). |
| **Jersey mismatches** | Numbers frequently differ across athletics sites, Ourlads, and media charts (shared numbers on large FBS rosters; media vs official conflicts). Prefer the school athletics roster for jersey when sources conflict (explicitly noted for Baylor Lagway #, TCU LS, and others). |
| **Position-battle volatility** | Extensive **OR** / co-starter listings (especially Arizona State, Iowa State, Kansas, Kansas State, Oklahoma State, West Virginia, UCF) mean Week-1 actual lineups may diverge from published boards. |
| **Roster churn / injuries** | Portal turnover, mid-camp injuries, and eligibility rulings can change counts after the research date. |
| **Hometown / HS quirks** | Occasional official-site anomalies (blank HS fields, mismatched city/school pairs, spelling variants) are preserved as published rather than “corrected.” |
| **Texas / Oklahoma exclusion** | Texas and Oklahoma football compete in the SEC and are omitted from this package. |
| **2024 expansion cohort** | Arizona, Arizona State, Colorado, and Utah are in their **third** Big 12 football season (joined 2024). |

## 5. How to Read the Attached Team Files

Each school file in `/workspace/big12-2026/` follows a consistent research structure:

1. **Header** — Conference affiliation, head coach (when stated), access/research date, OFFICIAL vs PROJECTED marking.
2. **Sources table** — URLs and access dates for every primary source used for that school.
3. **Starter Depth Chart** — Offense, defense, and specialists, with an explicit **projected vs official** caveat at the top of the section.
4. **Full Roster** — Player tables with columns for Name, Stars, Class Year, Position, High School / Hometown, and Notes (when present). Gaps are preserved as **Not listed**.
5. **Data gaps / caveats** — School-specific limitations (injuries, eligibility murk, jersey conflicts, OR competitions, etc.).

### Reading conventions

- Depth-chart **Status** labels (**OFFICIAL**, **MIXED**, **PROJECTED**) appear at the top of each school section in the starter summary.
- **Not listed** means the field was not verified — it does **not** mean “unrated” in a recruiting-service sense, nor that the player is unimportant.
- **Class Year** may mean academic eligibility (Fr/So/Jr/Sr / R-Sr / 5th / Gr / Post-Bacc / CU 1st–6th year) as published on athletics rosters, or HS recruiting/signing year when that is how the school file recorded it — see each file’s roster preamble.
- Prefer **official athletics spelling** when media spellings diverge (documented in individual caveats).
- Starter-summary enrichment pulls Stars / Class Year / HS-Hometown **only** from that school’s full roster table when a name match is found; unmatched depth-chart names retain **Not listed** gaps.
- **Texas** and **Oklahoma** are omitted (SEC members; no files in this package).
- **Arizona / Arizona State / Colorado / Utah** joined the Big 12 in **2024** (third conference football season in 2026).
