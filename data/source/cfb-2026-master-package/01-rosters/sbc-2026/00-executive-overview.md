## 2. Methodology and Primary Source Types

Research for each team was compiled from publicly available sources accessed on or about **September 5, 2026**. Primary source types used across the conference package:

1. **Official athletics department rosters** (school `.edu` / athletics sites) — primary authority for jersey numbers, names, positions, hometowns/high schools, and academic eligibility class when published.
2. **Official or beat-reported depth charts** — e.g., Coastal Carolina Week-1 PDF vs WVU; Southern Miss Week-1 game notes / Hattiesburg American; Troy Week-1 chart via al.com; ULM Week-1 estimated depth chart in game notes; South Alabama Week-1 release (paywalled partial); Appalachian State / Georgia Southern QB naming releases.
3. **Ourlads.com** depth charts and numeric/alphabetical rosters — secondary depth and hometown cross-checks (update stamps vary by school; often July–September 2026).
4. **Beat / local media depth charts and coaching comments** — e.g., JMU Sports News, WJCL/ESPN (Georgia Southern), Ruston Daily Leader (Louisiana Tech), The Advocate (Louisiana), NCFootballNews / Ashe Post & Times (Appalachian State), HERO Sports previews.
5. **Recruiting databases and class trackers** — 247Sports, On3, ESPN recruiting, CFBTrack, school signing-day releases — used **only** when a specific rating was publicly verified for a named player.
6. **Secondary cross-checks** — ESPN / CBS roster pages, Wikipedia season pages (portal/schedule / realignment context), school game notes PDFs where cited in team files.

**Policy enforced throughout:** If a recruiting star, high-school class year, or starter designation could not be verified in the sources consulted, the field is marked **Not listed** or the chart is labeled **projected**. No ratings or starter assignments were invented.

## 3. Cross-Conference Caveats

Readers should treat the attached materials as a **research snapshot**, not as an official Sun Belt Conference or NCAA publication. The following limitations apply conference-wide:

| Caveat | Implication |
| --- | --- |
| **Stars incomplete for transfers and upperclassmen** | Composite / consensus stars are filled primarily for verified 2026 (and some earlier) high-school signees and a minority of returners/transfers with published bios. Most portal arrivals and multi-year veterans are **Not listed**. |
| **Many depth charts are projected or mixed, not fully official** | **OFFICIAL:** Coastal Carolina, Southern Miss, Troy, ULM. **MIXED:** Appalachian State, Georgia Southern, Louisiana Tech. **PROJECTED:** Georgia State, James Madison, Marshall, Old Dominion, Arkansas State, Louisiana, South Alabama. |
| **Jersey mismatches** | Numbers frequently differ across athletics sites, Ourlads, and media charts (shared numbers on large FBS rosters; media vs official conflicts). Prefer the school athletics roster for jersey when sources conflict. |
| **Position-battle volatility** | Extensive **OR** / co-starter listings (especially Coastal Carolina, Southern Miss, Troy, ULM) mean Week-1 actual lineups may diverge from published boards. |
| **Roster churn / injuries** | Portal turnover, mid-camp injuries (documented in team files, e.g., Arkansas State Devin Spencer INJ; Louisiana TE Brock Chappell out for opener), and eligibility rulings can change counts after the research date. |
| **Hometown / HS quirks** | Occasional official-site anomalies (blank HS fields, mismatched city/school pairs, spelling variants) are preserved as published rather than “corrected.” |
| **Louisiana Tech / Texas State** | Louisiana Tech is **new to Sun Belt West** (2026). Texas State is a **Pac-12** member in 2026 and is omitted from this package. |
| **Paywalled official charts** | South Alabama released an official Week-1 chart (al.com, Sept 1) whose full text was not freely available at research time; the research file therefore labels depth **PROJECTED**. |
| **JMU non-release** | James Madison does not publish an official depth chart; starter boards in this package are **PROJECTED** (beat lean + Ourlads), including an unresolved QB1 conflict. |

## 5. How to Read the Attached Team Files

Each school file in `/workspace/sbc-2026/` follows a consistent research structure:

1. **Header** — Conference / division affiliation, head coach, home venue (when stated), access/research date.
2. **Sources table** — URLs and access dates for every primary source used for that school.
3. **Starter Depth Chart** — Offense, defense, and specialists, with an explicit **projected vs official** caveat at the top of the section.
4. **Full Roster** — Player tables with columns for Name, Stars, Class Year, Position, High School / Hometown, and Notes (when present). Gaps are preserved as **Not listed**.
5. **Data gaps / caveats** — School-specific limitations (injuries, eligibility murk, jersey conflicts, OR competitions, realignment notes, etc.).

### Reading conventions

- Depth-chart **Status** labels (**OFFICIAL**, **MIXED**, **PROJECTED**) appear at the top of each school section in the starter summary.
- **Not listed** means the field was not verified — it does **not** mean “unrated” in a recruiting-service sense, nor that the player is unimportant.
- **Class Year** may mean academic eligibility (Fr/So/Jr/Sr / R-Sr / 5th / 6th / Gr / Nth Year) as published on athletics rosters, or HS recruiting/signing year when that is how the school file recorded it — see each file’s roster preamble.
- Prefer **official athletics spelling** when media spellings diverge (documented in individual caveats).
- Starter-summary enrichment pulls Stars / Class Year / HS-Hometown **only** from that school’s full roster table when a name match is found; unmatched depth-chart names retain **Not listed** gaps.
- **Louisiana** denotes the University of Louisiana at Lafayette (**Ragin’ Cajuns**) — **not** Louisiana Tech or ULM.
- **Louisiana Tech** is the new **West Division** member (2026); **Texas State** is omitted (Pac-12).
- Master volume and starter summary are ordered **East Division (A–Z)** then **West Division (A–Z)**.
