## 2. Methodology and Primary Source Types

Research for each team was compiled from publicly available sources accessed on or about **September 4–5, 2026**. Primary source types used across the conference package:

1. **Official athletics department rosters** (school `.edu` / athletics sites) — primary authority for jersey numbers, names, positions, hometowns/high schools, and academic eligibility class when published.
2. **Official or beat-reported depth charts** — e.g., Boston College Week-1 PDF, Duke Week-1 chart, Florida State Week-0 game-day chart, Georgia Tech Week-1 game notes, NC State chart vs Virginia (PackInsider), Pitt Narduzzi Notebook, Syracuse Week-1 program two-deep, Virginia Week-0 chart, Wake Forest Week-1 program chart.
3. **Ourlads.com** depth charts and numeric/alphabetical rosters — secondary depth and hometown cross-checks (update stamps vary by school; often June–September 2026).
4. **Beat / local media depth charts and coaching comments** — e.g., Bear Insider (California), Greenville News / The State (Clemson), Cards On SI / Courier Journal (Louisville), Canes On SI (Miami), Inside Carolina / Heel Tough (North Carolina), Dave Campbell’s Texas Football / On3 (SMU), SI Stanford / Miami opener coverage, Virginia Tech On SI / Tech Sideline.
5. **Recruiting databases and class trackers** — 247Sports, On3, ESPN recruiting, CFBTrack, school signing-day releases — used **only** when a specific rating was publicly verified for a named player.
6. **Secondary cross-checks** — ESPN / CBS roster pages, Wikipedia season pages (portal/schedule context), school game notes PDFs where cited in team files.

**Policy enforced throughout:** If a recruiting star, high-school class year, or starter designation could not be verified in the sources consulted, the field is marked **Not listed** or the chart is labeled **projected**. No ratings or starter assignments were invented.

## 3. Cross-Conference Caveats

Readers should treat the attached materials as a **research snapshot**, not as an official Atlantic Coast Conference or NCAA publication. The following limitations apply conference-wide:

| Caveat | Implication |
| --- | --- |
| **Stars incomplete for transfers and upperclassmen** | Composite / consensus stars are filled primarily for verified 2026 (and some earlier) high-school signees and a minority of returners/transfers with published bios. Most portal arrivals and multi-year veterans are **Not listed**. |
| **Many depth charts are projected, not official** | Boston College, Duke, Florida State, Georgia Tech, NC State, Pittsburgh, Syracuse, Virginia, and Wake Forest have official / official-via-beat Week-1 (or Week-0) two-deeps. Virginia Tech is **MIXED** (coach-confirmed QB/OL; remainder projected). California, Clemson, Louisville, Miami (FL), North Carolina, SMU, and Stanford charts are primarily **PROJECTED** (Ourlads and/or beat). Clemson’s QB (Christopher Vizzina) is coach-confirmed even though the full chart is projected. |
| **Jersey mismatches** | Numbers frequently differ across athletics sites, Ourlads, and media charts (shared numbers on large FBS rosters; media vs official conflicts). Prefer the school athletics roster for jersey when sources conflict (explicitly noted for NC State and others). |
| **Position-battle volatility** | Extensive **OR** / co-starter listings (especially Boston College, Duke, Florida State, Georgia Tech, Pittsburgh) mean Week-1 actual lineups may diverge from published boards. |
| **Roster churn / injuries** | Portal turnover, mid-camp injuries, and eligibility rulings can change counts after the research date. |
| **Hometown / HS quirks** | Occasional official-site anomalies (blank HS fields, mismatched city/school pairs, spelling variants) are preserved as published rather than “corrected.” |
| **Notre Dame exclusion** | Notre Dame football is independent / not a full ACC football member and is omitted from this package. |
| **Miami naming** | **Miami (FL)** denotes the University of Miami Hurricanes (ACC) — **not** Miami (OH) / MAC. |

## 5. How to Read the Attached Team Files

Each school file in `/workspace/acc-2026/` follows a consistent research structure:

1. **Header** — Conference affiliation, head coach, home venue (when stated), access/research date.
2. **Sources table** — URLs and access dates for every primary source used for that school.
3. **Starter Depth Chart** — Offense, defense, and specialists, with an explicit **projected vs official** caveat at the top of the section.
4. **Full Roster** — Player tables with columns for Name, Stars, Class Year, Position, High School / Hometown, and Notes (when present). Gaps are preserved as **Not listed**.
5. **Data gaps / caveats** — School-specific limitations (injuries, eligibility murk, jersey conflicts, OR competitions, etc.).

| `boston-college.md` … `wake-forest.md` | Individual school research files (source of truth for player-level detail) |

### Reading conventions

- Depth-chart **Status** labels (**OFFICIAL**, **MIXED**, **PROJECTED**) appear at the top of each school section in the starter summary.
- **Not listed** means the field was not verified — it does **not** mean “unrated” in a recruiting-service sense, nor that the player is unimportant.
- **Class Year** may mean academic eligibility (Fr/So/Jr/Sr / R-Sr / 5th / Gr / Post-Bacc) as published on athletics rosters, or HS recruiting/signing year when that is how the school file recorded it — see each file’s roster preamble.
- Prefer **official athletics spelling** when media spellings diverge (documented in individual caveats).
- Starter-summary enrichment pulls Stars / Class Year / HS-Hometown **only** from that school’s full roster table when a name match is found; unmatched depth-chart names retain **Not listed** gaps.
- **Miami (FL)** denotes the University of Miami — ACC — **not** Miami University (Oxford, Ohio / MAC).
- **Notre Dame** is omitted (not a full ACC football member; no file in this package).
