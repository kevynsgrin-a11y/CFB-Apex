## Context: FCS → FBS transition

Sacramento State is a **new MAC football-only member** for 2026, transitioning from FCS (Big Sky era; 33 years FCS 1993–2025) to FBS. The Hornets replace Northern Illinois in the MAC for football. Non-football sports remain outside the MAC football alignment as described in school/conference announcements; football joined for a multi-year term beginning July 1, 2026.

**Eligibility / postseason caveat:** In June 2026 the NCAA Division I Cabinet eliminated the prior two-year postseason ban for FCS-to-FBS transitioning teams. Sacramento State is therefore **immediately eligible** for bowl games, the MAC Championship, and potentially the CFP if it meets the usual FBS criteria (typically at least 6–6). Entry costs reported around ~$23–24M over five years ($5M NCAA + ~$18M MAC).

**Coaching / scheme notes:** OC Eric Kiesau; DC Adam Clark; Ourlads lists Multiple offense / 3-4 defense (Wikipedia also notes 3–3–5 base). Large portal class under Carter after Brennan Marion left for Colorado.
