1. **Recruiting stars:** Complete 247Sports/On3 composite stars available mainly for 2025–2026 HS signees; most upperclassmen and portal transfers listed as **"Not listed"** (no reliable public composite found for this research pass). Where shown, ratings are 247Sports composite-style (e.g., 0.87x ≈ 3★).
2. **No official school depth chart** published on ubbulls.com at research time — starter designations are Ourlads/HERO, not UB Athletics.
3. **Jersey number collisions** are intentional (offense/defense share numbers).
4. **Class year labels** follow the athletics site (Fr./So./Jr./Sr./5th/6th); "Recruitment/class year" is inferred from standing + transfer history, not a signed LOI year for every player.
5. Ourlads HC line historically lagged (search snippets once showed Maurice Linguist); current Ourlads page correctly lists **Pete Lembo**.
6. Some freshmen (e.g., Blake Sholder) lack major/HS detail beyond athletics listing.
