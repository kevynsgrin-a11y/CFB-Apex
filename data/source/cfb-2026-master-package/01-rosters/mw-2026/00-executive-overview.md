## 2. Methodology and Primary Source Types

Research for each team was compiled from publicly available sources accessed on or about **September 4–5, 2026**. Primary source types used across the conference package:

1. **Official athletics department rosters** (school `.edu` / athletics sites) — primary authority for jersey numbers, names, positions, hometowns/high schools, and academic eligibility class when published.
2. **Official or beat-reported depth charts** — e.g., Colorado Springs Gazette (Air Force), Nevada Sports Net / WKU game notes (Nevada), Albuquerque Journal (New Mexico), NDSU game notes PDF (North Dakota State), Wyoming coach/beat confirmations.
3. **Ourlads.com** depth charts and numeric/alphabetical rosters — secondary depth and hometown cross-checks (update stamps vary by school; often June–September 2026).
4. **Beat / local media depth charts and coaching comments** — e.g., Honolulu Star-Advertiser (Hawaiʻi), SF Chronicle / ScoutSZN / Mountain West Connection (San José State), Dice City Sports / Review-Journal (UNLV), Shaw Local / Gazette (Northern Illinois), Wyoming News / PokesNews.
5. **Recruiting databases and class trackers** — 247Sports, On3, ESPN recruiting, CFBTrack, school signing-day releases — used **only** when a specific rating was publicly verified for a named player.
6. **Secondary cross-checks** — CBS Sports team rosters, Wikipedia season pages (portal/schedule context), TexasFootball.com (UTEP).

**Policy enforced throughout:** If a recruiting star, high-school class year, or starter designation could not be verified in the sources consulted, the field is marked **Not listed** or the chart is labeled **projected**. No ratings or starter assignments were invented.

## 3. Cross-Conference Caveats

Readers should treat the attached materials as a **research snapshot**, not as an official Mountain West or NCAA publication. The following limitations apply conference-wide:

| Caveat | Implication |
| --- | --- |
| **Stars incomplete for transfers and upperclassmen** | Composite / consensus stars are filled primarily for verified 2026 (and some earlier) high-school signees and a minority of returners/transfers with published bios. Most portal arrivals and multi-year veterans are **Not listed**. |
| **Many depth charts are projected, not official** | Nevada, New Mexico, and North Dakota State have strong official / official-via-beat Week-1 two-deeps. Air Force skill positions are Gazette-official for the opener; OL often preseason. Hawaiʻi, UTEP, and San José State charts are primarily projected. Northern Illinois, UNLV, and Wyoming are mixed (some confirmed starters; remainder projected). |
| **Jersey mismatches** | Numbers frequently differ across athletics sites, Ourlads, and media charts (shared numbers on large FBS rosters; media vs official conflicts). Prefer the school athletics roster for jersey when sources conflict. |
| **Position-battle volatility** | Open competitions (e.g., New Mexico QB OR; Air Force PK OR / LT competition; Northern Illinois three-way QB OR; Wyoming backup QB) mean Week-1 actual lineups may diverge from mid/late-August projections. |
| **Roster churn / injuries** | Portal turnover, season-ending injuries (documented for Air Force, UNLV center Boyd, Hawaiʻi Ashlock, etc.), and eligibility rulings can change counts after the research date. |
| **Hometown / HS quirks** | Occasional official-site anomalies (blank HS fields, mismatched city/school pairs, spelling variants) are preserved as published rather than “corrected.” |
| **FBS transition (NDSU)** | North Dakota State’s first FBS / MW season includes championship/bowl eligibility limits documented in the NDSU team file. |

## 5. How to Read the Attached Team Files

Each school file in `/workspace/mw-2026/` follows a consistent research structure:

1. **Header** — Conference affiliation, head coach, home venue (when stated), access/research date.
2. **Sources table** — URLs and access dates for every primary source used for that school.
3. **Starter Depth Chart** — Offense, defense, and specialists, with an explicit **projected vs official** caveat at the top of the section.
4. **Full Roster** — Player tables with columns for Name, Stars, Class Year, Position, High School / Hometown, and Notes. Gaps are preserved as **Not listed**.
5. **Data gaps / caveats** — School-specific limitations (injuries, eligibility murk, jersey conflicts, FBS transition notes, etc.).

### Reading conventions

- Depth-chart **Status** labels (**OFFICIAL**, **MIXED**, **PROJECTED**) appear at the top of each school section in the starter summary.
- **Not listed** means the field was not verified — it does **not** mean “unrated” in a recruiting-service sense, nor that the player is unimportant.
- **Class Year** may mean academic eligibility (Fr/So/Jr/Sr / R-Sr / 5th) as published on athletics rosters, or HS recruiting/signing year when that is how the school file recorded it — see each file’s roster preamble.
- Prefer **official athletics spelling** when media spellings diverge (documented in individual caveats).
- Starter-summary enrichment pulls Stars / Class Year / HS-Hometown **only** from that school’s full roster table when a name match is found; unmatched depth-chart names retain **Not listed** gaps.
