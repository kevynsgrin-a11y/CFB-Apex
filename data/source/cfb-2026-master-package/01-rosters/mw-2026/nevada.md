## Data gaps / caveats
- Stars: 247Sports composite rating converted to star bands (90+=4★, 80–89=3★, 70–79=2★). **66/102** players have a published 247 rating on the team roster page; remainder marked **Not listed** (common for JuCo/FCS/walk-on/international/older transfers).
- Class year column mixes **roster academic year** (Fr/So/Jr/Sr/5th/6th) with HS recruiting class or transfer path when known; exact original HS class year is Not listed for many transfers.
- Don Henry hometown listed as Houma, La. with Hesperia, Calif. / Victor Valley College path on athletics site (bio wording varies).
- Ourlads (updated earlier in summer) may list names/weights that differ slightly from the Sep 2026 official athletics roster; athletics site is treated as primary for presence and jersey.
- Depth chart is Week 1 opener chart and can change weekly.
- No invention: missing recruiting stars, HS for Aussie specialists, and some transfer HS class years are explicitly Not listed.
