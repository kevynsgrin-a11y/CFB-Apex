## Data gaps / caveats

1. **Recruiting stars:** Air Force does not release official signing lists (appointment process). 247Sports/On3 coverage is sparse; majority of players = **Not listed**. Known 3★ marks above are from 247Sports commit pages / Gazette reporting for 2024–2026 classes.  
2. **HS recruiting class year vs academic year:** Table uses official academic year (Fr/So/Jr/Sr). Academy timing (prep school, turnbacks) can desync HS class from eligibility year.  
3. **Depth chart OL:** Opener OL starters not fully confirmed in Gazette narrative; preseason Media Days chart used.  
4. **Hometown/HS conflicts:** Adam Hrynciw — official site Fremont Ross (OH); Ourlads lists Christopher/Gilroy, CA. Official preferred.  
5. **Position dual-listings:** Kemper Hodges, Jet Wiley, Keegan Sack, Mason Lowe, Samuel Rhoades V appear at different positions across official roster vs depth charts.  
6. **True freshmen 2026 class:** Many 2026 signees (Knight Wilson, Case Vanden Bosch, etc.) may be at prep/not yet on varsity roster; not inventoried here unless on official roster.  
7. **Stars methodology:** Where 247Sports Composite ~0.80–0.89 → listed as 3★; exact star graphic not always visible.
