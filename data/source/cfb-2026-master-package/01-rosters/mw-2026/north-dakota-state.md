## Data gaps / caveats

1. **Most NDSU players lack public 247/On3 star ratings** historically (FCS-era recruiting profile). Only a subset of the 2026 HS class and a few transfers have published stars; remainder marked **Not listed**.
2. **Depth chart is official as of Aug. 26, 2026** opener notes — may have shifted after Jacksonville State / into Fordham week; Ourlads 8/28 still aligned.
3. **Jersey duplicates** (e.g., #0, #2, #7, #55) are normal offense/defense sharing on the published roster.
4. **Recruiting class vs. roster:** Signing-day releases list some names (e.g., late signee reports of Luke Tetzlaff / Roman Johnson in beat media) that did **not** appear on the live GoBison roster at access — not included.
5. **Hometown spelling variants** (e.g., Preston Brown Hillsboro/Festus; Henry Nelson Boise/Meridian; Q Barnslater Apple Valley/Burnsville) — HS + primary hometown from official roster preferred.
6. **FBS transition:** First Mountain West / FBS season; Summit League for other sports; wrestling Big 12 — football-only MW affiliate.
7. Ourlads and game notes occasionally differ on OL interior labels (e.g., Magin listed OT on roster / RG on depth) — **depth-chart position follows official notes**.
