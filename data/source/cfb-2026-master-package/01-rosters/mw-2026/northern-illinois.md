## Data gaps / caveats

1. **Recruiting stars incomplete for most of the roster.** Public 247/On3/ESPN ratings cover mainly the 2026 high-school signing class (mostly 3★) and a few older commits. Portal / FCS / D2 / JUCO transfers and many returning upperclassmen have **Not listed** — this is a source gap, not an invention.
2. **QB starter unsettled as of research date.** Team depth chart used OR among Macon / Dickens / Davidson for the Iowa opener week; Ourlads (8/20) had Dickens first — treat as **fluid**.
3. **No NIU Athletics HTML “official depth chart” page** was found as a stable public URL; two-deep relies on Ourlads + beat reporting of Harley’s released chart.
4. **Jersey number collisions** appear on the official roster (e.g., multiple #0, #2, #5) — offense/defense sharing; listed as published.
5. **Some secondary sources (ESPN/CBS/247)** lag or omit players vs. niuhuskies.com — **official athletics page is authoritative for names/HS/hometown**.
6. **Mountain West / coaching context:** First MWC season; interim HC Rob Harley after prior staff changes; large transfer influx reported (~29 in beat coverage).
7. **Troy Garner** appears on CFBTrack 2026 board but was **not** on the live official roster page at access time — not added to full roster.
8. Spelling: official roster lists **Ross Odgen** (not Ogden); **Jaidaun Cranford**; **Taron Dickens** (beat sometimes “Tyger”).
