- Name spelling: athletics uses **Ormanie** Arnold; Journal uses Ormaine. Athletics **Kiefer Sibley**; Wikipedia transfer table Keifer Seibley. Used athletics spelling.
- Position labels: Journal lists Jayden Flaker and Nick Wheeler in secondary/LB roles that differ from athletics primary position labels — noted in Notes.
- Gabriel Wilson (CFBTrack #1 2026 RB commit) does not appear on the Sep 2026 official athletics roster; not included.
- Walter Moore (CFBTrack EDGE) not clearly matched to Trey Moore TE on roster — Trey Moore stars left Not listed.
- Depth chart is Week 1 and fluid (especially QB). Jack Layne injured and listed as reserve/INJ on Ourlads.
- Never invented missing stars, HS, or hometowns.
