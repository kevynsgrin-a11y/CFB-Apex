## Completeness summary

| Category | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Head coach, OC, DC (or co-DC) for all 14 | **Complete** (South Alabama: no separate titled OC — HC Major Applewhite calls offense) |
| Special teams coordinator | **Nearly complete** — Troy lists special-teams analyst / assistant only (**Not listed** as dedicated STC); elsewhere titled STC (or dual-titled STC) located |
| QB / RB / WR position coaches | **Strong** for most programs; **Louisiana** has no titled QB coach (Senior Offensive Analyst / QB support only); **Arkansas State** has no separate titled QB coach beyond OC Garrett Altman |
| Offensive scheme labels | **Mixed** — several clear attributions (Coastal Petrino system; JMU up-tempo spread; Troy Veer & Shoot; Louisiana Tech Air Raid; Marshall multiple / Rodriguez-tree spread-option; App State Briles-tempo lineage); others carefully attributed or **Not listed** when no official brand appears |
| Defensive scheme labels | **Mixed** — documented bases include Coastal **4-2-5**, Georgia Southern aggressive **3-4** / nickel-heavy, Louisiana Tech **3-3-5**, Southern Miss **4-2-5**, South Alabama multiple **4-2-5**, ULM **3-4** (beat attribution); Marshall multiple (3-/4-man fronts); several others **Not listed** |

**Major gaps:** (1) Dedicated special-teams title at **Troy**. (2) Titled QB coach at **Louisiana** and **Arkansas State**. (3) Single official offensive “brand” labels for Georgia Southern, Georgia State, Old Dominion, Louisiana, Arkansas State, and South Alabama remain thin in public sources. (4) Single official defensive base tags for Appalachian State, Georgia State, James Madison, Old Dominion, Arkansas State, Louisiana, and Troy are incomplete or multiple-only in sources consulted.

## Notes on conflicts and dual titles

1. **Dual-role coordinators** are common (OC/QB, DC/LB, STC/position). Titles are reported as published; rooms are not artificially split when athletics lists a single combined title.
2. **South Alabama** is the clearest HC-as-play-caller case: Applewhite has no separate titled OC for 2026.
3. **Louisiana Tech** WR room lists **two** titled wide receivers coaches (Joe and Graham); both are recorded.
4. **Georgia Southern** and **Southern Miss** publish split outside/inside receiver rooms; both names are recorded under WR.
5. **Old Dominion** January 2026 release named Wendy Laurent at tight ends; the current 2026 athletics roster/coach block lists **Ryan Wilson** at tight ends. This report follows the live athletics roster for on-field titles and does not invent a WR/TE conflict for the requested fields.
6. Scheme labels prefer **athletics, Ourlads, game notes, or on-record coach quotes**. When only flexible / multiple language exists without a published exclusive base, the entry is **Not listed**.
