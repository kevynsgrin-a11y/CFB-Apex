### Research limitations (~Sept 5, 2026)

1. Several Sidearm coaches pages (BGSU, Buffalo, Toledo) returned truncated or support-staff-only HTML via automated fetch; staff were reconstituted from roster coaching blocks, game notes PDFs, Wikipedia 2026 team pages, and beat reporting.
2. **Never invent** rule: exclusive front/scheme labels are marked **Not listed** unless athletics, media guides/game notes, Ourlads, or named beat sources publish them.
3. Title overlaps (HC as OC; co-coordinators; pass-game coordinator without OC title) are preserved as published rather than inferred into missing titles.
4. Sacramento State’s FBS transition and several 2025–26 coordinator changes (Buffalo, Toledo, EMU, WMU DC, UMass OC, Miami OC) leave more scheme gaps than stable staffs.
