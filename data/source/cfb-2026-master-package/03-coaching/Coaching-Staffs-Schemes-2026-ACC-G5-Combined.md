## Methodology

- **Primary preference:** Official athletics coaching directories, roster/coaching-staff blocks, and school media guides / game notes for the 2026 season.
- **Cross-checks:** Wikipedia 2026 team pages and the FBS coaches compilation; CBS Sports Power Four coordinator-hire coverage (ACC); credible beat and national reporting (e.g., Underdog Dynasty AAC, ByTheBelt MAC, Ourlads depth charts, SI, Commercial Appeal, al.com, Star-Advertiser, Review-Journal, and conference-specific beat outlets named in each PART).
- **Conflict handling:** When athletics CMS pages, roster PDFs, Wikipedia, and beat reporting disagree, official athletics directories are preferred for on-field titles; material conflicts are noted in the conference completeness sections (examples: Northern Illinois Wikipedia vs. niuhuskies.com OC/WR titles; Jacksonville State Wikipedia vs. Ourlads defensive labels; Louisville incomplete gocards.com CMS fetch).
- **“Not listed” standard:** Applied when no reliable 2026 primary source named a discrete role or a formal scheme / base-front label. Historical résumé language or flexible/multiple descriptions are not converted into a single invented base tag.
- **Access / research window:** On or about **September 5, 2026 (PT)**. Midseason staff changes after that date are outside this snapshot.

## Common gaps (cross-conference)

These patterns appear repeatedly in the six source reports and should be treated as structural limits of the public record, not as omissions of available names:

1. **Special teams titles**  
   Dedicated STC titles are strong in the ACC (complete for all 17) and generally strong in AAC / CUSA / MW, but gaps and title ambiguity remain — e.g., **Kent State** (special-teams analyst only), **Troy** (analyst / assistant only; no dedicated STC), **San José State** (Fred Guidici listed as Special Teams Analyst, not STC), and **Missouri State** (special-teams duties split across multiple titles with no single STC). Several STCs are dual-titled with position rooms (STC/RBs, STC/safeties, UTEP “We-fense,” etc.).

2. **Dual-role offensive coordinators (and HC play-callers)**  
   Many programs publish OC / Quarterbacks (or Co-OC / QBs) as a single title rather than separate rooms. A smaller set has **no separate titled OC**: **Boston College** (HC Bill O’Brien oversees offense), **Florida Atlantic** (Kittley / Owl Raid play-caller), **North Texas** (Neal Brown), **UAB** (Alex Mortensen), **Akron** (Joe Moorhead play-caller), **Ball State** (Mike Uremovich; no formal OC title), and **South Alabama** (Major Applewhite). Dual titles are reported as stated, not artificially split.

3. **QB room title gaps**  
   Discrete primary QB coach titles are missing or soft in places such as **FAU** (assistant QB only), **Tulsa** (Barbay OC without QB title), **UMass** (Warner OC without paired QB title), **Sam Houston** (Longo play-calls; no dedicated QB coach), **Louisiana**, and **Arkansas State** (OC covers the room). Entries use **Not listed** rather than inventing a QB coach of record.

4. **Flexible defensive fronts often unlabeled**  
   Where staffs describe multiple, hybrid, or personnel-driven fronts without a single official “base defense” line, entries are **Not listed** (or carefully attributed as multiple). Documented bases appear for subsets in each league (see scheme themes below); large shares of ACC, AAC, MAC, MW, CUSA, and Sun Belt defenses remain unlabeled as an exclusive front.

Additional conference-specific gaps (CMS truncation, Wikipedia lag, realignment membership notes) are retained in each PART’s completeness section rather than restated here.

## Brief scheme themes (evident in sources only)

Themes below summarize language already present in the conference files. No new scheme assignments are added.

- **ACC:** Documented offensive identities include Miami **Air Raid–based**, California **pro-style**, Clemson Morris **two-back / play-action**, Georgia Tech **pro spread**, Virginia / Wake Forest **spread**, and UNC Petrino **tempo spread** (hire coverage). Documented defensive bases include Georgia Tech / Virginia multiple **4–2–5**, Wake Forest **4–2–5**, and Florida State Tony White **3–3–5** / three-safety; most other ACC defenses remain **Not listed** as a single official base.
- **AAC:** Option lineage remains visible at Army and Navy (**Wing-T / option** hybrid); FAU **Owl Raid**; Memphis Decker **spread/tempo** with Guidry **4-2-5**; Rice **gun-option**; Tulane **West Coast / spread “Hall Ball”**; Temple **3-4** multiple; Charlotte **Spread Option** / **4-2-5** (Ourlads). Most other exclusive defensive bases are **Not listed**.
- **MAC:** Stronger offensive sourcing includes Akron Moorhead multi-tempo RPO / West Coast, Central Michigan **power spread**, Ohio **spread-option / RPO**, UMass **multiple pro-style**, and Western Michigan’s run-physical evolving balance. Defensive labels in-source include CMU even-front / Ourlads **4-3**, EMU **4-2-5**, Kent State prior **4-3**, and UMass Ourlads **4-3 / 4-2-5**; many others remain unlabeled.
- **Mountain West:** Explicit brands include Air Force **triple-option / flexbone**, Hawaiʻi **run-and-shoot** (+ Air Raid / RPO wrinkles), Nevada **Air Raid–inspired**, San José State **Spread & Shred**, UTEP **Blue Blaze** / **Orange Swarm** / **We-fense**, and NDSU Wikipedia **Multiple pro-style** / **Multiple 4–3**. Hawaiʻi preview language cites **4-2-5**; several other defenses are multiple / personnel-driven without an exclusive official base.
- **CUSA:** High-confidence offensive labels include Delaware **spread option**, Kennesaw State **Veer and Shoot** (beat), Liberty Chadwell **option-based**, Sam Houston / WKU **Air Raid**, and NMSU Ourlads **Multiple**. Defensive bases with stronger sourcing include Delaware / NMSU / WKU **3–3–5**; Jacksonville State shows Wikipedia **4–3** vs. Ourlads **3–3–5** conflict (disclosed, not silently resolved).
- **Sun Belt (East/West):** Documented offensive identities include Coastal Petrino-family attack, JMU up-tempo **spread**, Troy **Veer & Shoot**, Louisiana Tech **Air Raid**, Marshall multiple / Rodriguez-tree **spread-option**, App State Briles-tempo lineage, and South Alabama Ourlads **multiple spread**. Documented defensive bases include Coastal **4-2-5**, Georgia Southern aggressive **3-4**, Louisiana Tech **3-3-5**, Southern Miss **4-2-5**, South Alabama multiple **4-2-5**, and ULM **3-4** (beat attribution).
