# Big Ten Conference Football Coaching Staffs & Schemes — 2026 Season

**Access / research date:** September 5, 2026 (PT)  
**Scope:** All 18 Big Ten programs  
**Method:** Prefer official athletics staff directories and team releases; cross-check with Wikipedia FBS coaches table (updated through 2025 season / current listings), CBS Sports / ESPN coordinator-hire coverage, and beat reporting. Where a role or scheme is not named in primary sources, the entry is **Not listed** (nothing invented).

## Completeness & gaps (summary)

| Area | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Head coach / OC / DC | Complete for all 18 (Washington: no separate titled OC; Jedd Fisch calls plays) |
| Special teams coordinator | Complete for all 18 (primary title may be dual-role) |
| QB / RB / WR coaches | Complete or dual-titled for nearly all; UCLA has no stand-alone titled QB coach (OC oversees QBs; assistant QB listed) |

| Primary gaps | Exact front labels for Maryland, Michigan State, Minnesota, Northwestern, Ohio State, Oregon, Purdue, Rutgers, UCLA, Washington, Wisconsin (described where reporting allows; otherwise **Not listed**); Minnesota co-OC Matt Simon appears on Wikipedia but not on the official 2026 coaches table used here; Washington special-teams title differs between Wikipedia (Jordan Paopao) and official UW directory (Chris Petrilli) — **official directory preferred** |

## Cross-reference notes

1. **Dual titles are common** (OC/QB, OC/WR, DC/LBs, ST/TE). Where dual, both roles are shown; the named coach is counted for both columns.  
2. **Washington** is the only Big Ten program without a separately titled offensive coordinator for 2026.  
3. **UCLA** is the clearest gap on a dedicated QB coach title; practice/room leadership sits with OC Dean Kennedy plus assistant Jon Boyer.  
4. Scheme language prefers coach/media quotes over video-game databases; game-scheme tables are treated as secondary only when consistent with reporting.
