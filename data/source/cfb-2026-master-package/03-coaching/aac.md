### Structural / source gaps called out explicitly

1. **FAU, North Texas, UAB:** Official directories omit a named Offensive Coordinator; contemporaneous reporting attributes play-calling to the head coach. Entries use **Not listed** for the OC title rather than inventing a dual-role title the school did not publish.
2. **FAU QB / Tulsa QB:** No primary “Quarterbacks” coach line on the main 2026 coaching table; assistant/analyst titles or OC-only titles are noted without inventing a QB coach of record.
3. **Defensive base fronts:** Only Memphis (**4-2-5**), Charlotte (**4-2-5**, Ourlads), and Temple (**3-4**) had clear, attributable base labels in sources reviewed. Navy’s hybrid 3-4/3-5 is preview language. Most other AAC defenses remain **Not listed** for an exclusive base.
4. **UAB athletics coaches page:** Sidearm coaches directory returned incomplete coaching-staff HTML / staff-directory errors during fetch; UAB rows rely on hire releases, staff bios, al.com, Birmingham Banner, and Underdog Dynasty.
5. **Temple coaches page:** Full coaching table partially truncated in automated fetch; OC/DC/ST/RB/WR confirmed via Wikipedia 2026 page, roster/coaches fragments, spring blog, and beat reporting.
6. **ECU coaches page:** Support-staff table rendered cleanly; coaching-staff rows reconstructed from WebSearch snapshot of the same official page plus hire releases for Soto/Hankins.
7. **UTSA:** `goutsa.com/sports/football/coaches` returned 404; 2026 roster page coaching-staff block and staff directory used instead.
8. **Schemes:** Prefer coach quotes and official branding (Owl Raid, Hall Ball, Decker spread, Army option, Navy Wing-T/option, Rice gun-option). Where only résumé history or soft preview language exists, the document states **Not listed** or “partial” rather than forcing a single invented tag.
