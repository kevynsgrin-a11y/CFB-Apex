# Mountain West Conference Football Coaching Staffs & Schemes — 2026 Season

**Research date:** September 5, 2026 (PT)  
**Scope:** All ten Mountain West football members (post-2026 realignment)  
**Method:** Prefer official athletics staff directories, roster coaching blocks, media guides / game notes, and contemporaneous beat reporting. Where a discrete role or formal scheme / base-front label was not published, the entry is **Not listed** (no invention).

**2026 membership context:** After Boise State, Colorado State, Fresno State, San Diego State, and Utah State departed for the Pac-12, the Mountain West retained Air Force, Nevada, New Mexico, San José State, UNLV, and Wyoming; Hawaiʻi became a full member; UTEP joined from Conference USA; Northern Illinois joined as a football-only member from the MAC; North Dakota State joined as a football-only member from the FCS / Missouri Valley Football Conference.

## Completeness & gaps (detailed)

### Staffing fields generally complete
Across all ten programs, **Head Coach**, **Offensive Coordinator** (or co-OC structure), and **QB / RB / WR** rooms are published on official athletics directories or roster coaching blocks for 2026, with the dual-title caveats noted above (OC also QB, OC also RB, Co-OC / WR, etc.).

### Discrete gaps (do not invent)
1. **Northern Illinois — Defensive Coordinator:** After Thomas Hammock’s February 2026 resignation and Rob Harley’s elevation from DC to **interim head coach**, the official NIU coaches table does **not** list a separate DC. Defensive position coaches remain (DT, DE, CB/pass defense, safeties; STC/LB).  
2. **San José State — Special Teams Coordinator:** Official listings show **Fred Guidici, Special Teams Analyst**, not a Special Teams Coordinator title.  
3. **Air Force RB room:** Split between Campbell (RB/slots) and Horton (fullbacks / STC) — both reported rather than forced into one “RB coach” invention.  
4. **UTEP special teams title styling:** Athletics CMS renders Pardee’s title with the program’s **“We-fense”** branding (special teams); treated as the special-teams coordinator equivalent.

### Scheme-label gaps
- Strongest explicit scheme brands in-source: Air Force **triple option**; Hawaiʻi **run-and-shoot**; Nevada **Air Raid–inspired**; San José State **Spread & Shred**; UTEP **Blue Blaze** / **Orange Swarm** / **We-fense**; NDSU Wikipedia **Multiple pro-style** / **Multiple 4–3**.  
- Weakest / unlabeled exclusive bases: Nevada defense, New Mexico defense, NIU defense, UNLV defense, UTEP technical front, Air Force defensive front, SJSU defensive front, Wyoming exclusive static front — marked **Not listed** or described only with attributed beat language.

### Source-quality notes
- Prefer athletics CMS / roster staff tables over Wikipedia when titles conflict (notably **Northern Illinois** OC/WR assignments).  
- NDSU athletics HTML fetch returned support staff cleanly; on-field coach names and scheme tags are corroborated via the Wikipedia 2026 team page coaches list (updated July 30, 2026) and prior gobison.com search index snapshots.  
- Research window: on or about **September 5, 2026 (PT)**. Midseason staff changes after that date are outside this snapshot.
