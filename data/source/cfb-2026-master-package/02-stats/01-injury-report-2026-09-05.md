# FBS College Football Injury Report

**As of:** September 5, 2026 (PT)  
**Scope:** FBS programs with publicly reported injuries or conference availability designations  
**Access date for all sources:** September 5, 2026 (PT)

---

## Executive summary

This report compiles **verified** player availability and injury information for FBS football as of Saturday, September 5, 2026 (Pacific Time). Entries are drawn from ESPN reporting, conference availability releases (notably the ACC), athletics/program statements, and credible beat coverage. Status labels follow published designations (Out, Doubtful, Questionable, Probable, Out for season, etc.) or the clearest equivalent language used by the source.

**Coverage note:** Most Power conferences mandate formal availability reports primarily for **conference** games. Big Ten midweek/gameday reports for league opponents do not begin until mid-September 2026. SEC and Big 12 policies similarly emphasize conference competition. Consequently, early-season (non-conference) weeks rely heavily on coach comments and beat reporting, and many FBS programs publish little or no comprehensive public list. Absence from this document is **not** evidence that a roster is healthy.

**Never invent:** Only injuries and statuses explicitly reported by the cited sources are included. Speculative depth-chart chatter without a stated status is omitted.

## Coverage gaps and methodology

1. **Conference availability timing:** ACC teams in conference play (e.g., FSU–SMU) published formal designations this week. Big Ten, SEC, and Big 12 formal midweek reports are largely inactive for non-conference openers; Big Ten’s expanded in-week reports begin with conference slate around Sept. 19, 2026.
2. **Group of Five / many Power programs:** No comprehensive public Week 1 lists were located for large swaths of the AAC, Mountain West, MAC, Sun Belt, Conference USA, or most Big 12/SEC/Big Ten rosters beyond the items above.
3. **Camp trackers vs. gameday:** The CBS Sports Power Four camp injury tracker (Aug. 25, 2026) informed several season-ending and multi-week cases; Week 1 statuses were cross-checked against later coach comments or beat updates where available (e.g., Texas Goosby cleared; Oklahoma Maikkula/Fodje Week 1 outs; South Carolina Stewart/Peak Week 1 outs).
4. **Roster departures ≠ injuries:** Items such as players no longer with a program are excluded unless an injury status was the reported cause of absence.
5. **Status language:** Where coaches used phrases such as “will not play,” “good to go,” or “game-time decision,” those are mapped to Out / Available–Probable / Questionable–GTD respectively and attributed.
